    I'm an undergraduate student at Islamic University of Technology (IUT). Currently I am affiliated with the <a href="https://cse.iutoic-dhaka.edu/ssl">Computer Vision Lab (CV)</a> research group of IUT, working on my undergraduate thesis under the supervision of <a href="https://cse.iutoic-dhaka.edu/profile/kushol/education">Dr Rafsanjany Kushol</a>.
    <br><br>
    My research interest lies in Medical Image Processing, Computer Vision, Vision Language Models and Deep Learning, Multimodal Large Language Models and Reinforcement Learning. At present, I am working on Ultra-Low to High Field MRI image processing techniques.
